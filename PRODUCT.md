# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Factory operators use the application on a single touch-screen industrial PC. Engineers, administrators, and developers configure products, cameras, OCR, ROI regions, users, and diagnostics.

## Product Purpose

Provide a reliable local-first desktop workflow for camera and PLC-driven OCR inspection, production counting, result history, and synchronization with the Dongil Server.

## Brand Personality

Clear, dependable, operational.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative SaaS styling, web-only interaction assumptions, hover-dependent controls, excessive cards, ambiguous status copy, and layouts that sacrifice the 1280 x 1024 factory viewport.

## Design Principles

- Make current machine state understandable at a glance.
- Keep operational controls touch-friendly and predictable.
- Prefer direct status feedback over decorative presentation.
- Preserve stable workflows and local operation during network loss.
- Use consistent English and Vietnamese terminology through the i18n layer.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Provide semantic controls, visible focus states, keyboard access, sufficient contrast, large touch targets, and status communication that does not rely on color alone.
